**Subject: Request for PhilSensors water-level and rainfall data access — Agno River basin / Dagupan City**

Dear Sir/Madam,

ClimateShield-Dagupan is a volunteer community project providing open flood-preparedness tools for Dagupan City, Pangasinan, in support of the City DRRM Office.

While integrating PhilSensors, we noted (public data page, 7 October 2026) that the Pangasinan water-level stations — including Calvo Bridge (Bayambang), Hector Mendoza Bridge (Alcala), Macalong and Tagamusing Bridges (Urdaneta), San Roque Dam, and Don Marcelo Nagal Bridge (Agno) — and the nearby rain gauges (Calasiao, Binmaley, PAGASA-Dagupan) show latest public readings between 2015 and 2024. No station currently reports inside Dagupan.

We respectfully request:

1. Information on the **operational status** of these stations and any **current** water-level/rain stations upstream of Dagupan (Agno, Sinocalan, Pantal–Calmay systems).
2. **Access to near-real-time readings** (an API or periodic export) for those stations, under your data-use terms, for display on a community dashboard with full attribution to DOST-ASTI and PhilSensors.
3. **Historical records** for the same stations for flood-scenario calibration.
4. Guidance on whether a **water-level station on the Pantal River** in Dagupan could be considered under ASTI's programs.
