ClimateShield-Dagupan is a volunteer, non-commercial community project producing flood-preparedness maps for Dagupan City in support of the City DRRM Office.

Our current terrain source, Copernicus GLO-30, records about 62% of Dagupan's land area at exactly 0 m (the fishpond and wetland belt is flattened), which makes flood-depth estimates coarse. We respectfully request:

1. **LiDAR-derived DTM (bare-earth), 1–5 m resolution**, covering Dagupan City (approx. 120.21–120.42° E, 15.96–16.22° N), in GeoTIFF; or **IfSAR DTM** if LiDAR is unavailable.
2. Metadata on **vertical datum** (MSL / ellipsoid) and acquisition year.
3. Any available **flood-hazard maps** for the Agno / Pantal floodplain derived from the same data.

Use: research and community flood-preparedness only; full attribution; no redistribution of the raw data. We will sign any required data-sharing agreement.
